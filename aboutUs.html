About Us

Home-About Us

Our Vision

Our vision is to provide a happy, caring and stimulating environment where children will recognize and achieve their fullest potential, so that they can make their best contribution to society.

Our vision is of a natural, holistic, student-centered learning environment that empowers and inspires our students to be creative, innovative, academic leaders.

Our Mission

Krishna Valley School, Chunar recognizes that each child is an individual, that all children are creative, and that all children need to succeed. Therefore, Krishna Valley School caters to the individual needs of children, fosters a caring and creative environment, and emphasizes the social, emotional, physical, and intellectual development of each child.

The mission of our school contributes to our vision by educating young leaders in global citizenship. Our purpose is to champion a new model of learning that connects the timeless lessons from nature to relevant and effective preparation for a fast-changing future.

To instill ethical values and develop exceptional students from diverse economic, social, and cultural backgrounds to serve the nation as wise and principled leaders who contribute to national scientific and technological development.

Krishna Valley School Seeks to Embody

  • Excellence in the classroom through experiential learning.
  • Inclusive education for all kinds of learners.
  • Pedagogy that responds to diverse learning styles.
  • A culture of innovative teaching strategies and continuous improvement.
  • Spiritual pursuits based on religious traditions, faith, and openness to all religions and customs.
  • The pursuit of truth, wisdom, freedom of inquiry, and a quest for excellence.
